Masala Paneer

Masala Paneer is a delicious Indian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich and spicy tomato-onion gravy. The blend of spices gives it a flavorful kick, making it perfect to enjoy with rice, naan, or roti. This dish is both easy to prepare and satisfying for paneer lovers.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams paneer, cut into cubes
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon ghee (optional)
  • 1 cup finely chopped onions
  • 1 cup finely chopped tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 to 1¼ cups water (adjust as needed)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Heat oil and ghee in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  4. Mix in ginger-garlic paste and sauté for a minute until aromatic.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil separates.
  6. Stir in turmeric, coriander powder, red chili powder, and salt.
  7. Pour in water and bring the gravy to a gentle boil.
  8. Add paneer cubes and simmer for 5–7 minutes to let the flavors blend.
  9. Sprinkle garam masala and chopped coriander leaves on top.
  10. Serve hot with rice, naan, or roti.
