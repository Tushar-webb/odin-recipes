Gajar Ka Halwa

Gajar Ka Halwa is a popular Indian dessert made with grated carrots, milk, sugar, and ghee. Slowly cooked until it becomes rich and flavorful, it’s garnished with nuts and raisins for extra texture and taste. Perfect for festivals, celebrations, or a comforting treat.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams grated carrots (about 4-5 medium carrots)
  • 4 cups full-fat milk
  • ½ cup sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 4 tablespoons ghee
  • 5-6 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • 10-12 cashews
  • 10-12 almonds, sliced
  • 10-12 raisins

Steps

  1. Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the grated carrots and sauté for 5-6 minutes until they soften slightly.
  3. Pour in the milk and bring it to a boil.
  4. Reduce the heat and cook on low, stirring frequently, until the milk reduces and the carrots are tender (about 45-50 minutes).
  5. Add sugar and crushed cardamom, and cook for another 5-10 minutes, stirring continuously.
  6. In a small pan, lightly fry the cashews, almonds, and raisins in a teaspoon of ghee.
  7. Mix the fried nuts and raisins into the halwa.
  8. Serve warm, garnished with extra nuts if desired.
