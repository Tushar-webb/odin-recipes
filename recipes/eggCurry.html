EggCurry

Egg Curry is a tasty Indian dish where boiled eggs are cooked in a spicy tomato and onion sauce. The spices make it flavorful and warm, and it goes well with rice or bread. It’s easy to make and perfect for a quick, hearty meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 to 5 boiled eggs
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon ghee (optional)
  • 1¼ cups finely chopped onions (about 2 medium onions)
  • 1 cup finely chopped tomatoes (about 3 medium tomatoes)
  • 1 green chili, chopped or slit (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste or finely minced ginger and garlic
  • ½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 to 1¼ cups water (adjust as needed)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Heat oil and ghee in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Mix in the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for another minute.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften and the oil begins to separate.
  5. Pour in water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes to allow the flavors to blend.
  7. Carefully add the boiled eggs to the curry.
  8. Sprinkle chopped coriander leaves over the curry.
  9. Serve hot with rice or roti.
